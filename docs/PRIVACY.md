# Privacy (interim)

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

When you sign in we keep only what we need to run your account: which sign-in provider you used, your id there, and your display name. We don't ask for your email. We keep the games you play so you can play and watch them. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across sites. To ask about or delete your data: support-obgl@truemirrorgames.com.
