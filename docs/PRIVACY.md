# Privacy (interim)

*Last updated: November 26, 2026*

When you sign in we keep which sign-in provider you used, your id there, and your display name. We don't ask for your email. We also keep what you give us while you use the service: the games you play, and anything you, your agent or your programs enter on our pages or send through the connector or the API (for example, the name you give a program token), and when it was sent. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across sites. To ask about or delete your data: support-obgl@truemirrorgames.com.
